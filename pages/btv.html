BEYOND THE VEIL

A story-driven top-down game where the universe holds more than it shows.

Synopsis:

Elara and her husband, Alistair, have always been on top of things at Novum Physica, a private scientific research company dedicated to the cosmos and its mysteries. One day they were tasked with a confidential project by the owner of the company, Erina. Little did they know that this project would bring about a chain of events that would change their perspective on everything they thought they knew.

As of now:

The game is in its planning phase! Many of the main components of the game have been planned out. The direction of the game has also been decided. A prototype is being worked on to be able to visualize these things and move forwards with the current plans.

Where I currently stand, I am the sole developer of this game. I will consider a team down the path, but for now, everything falls on me. I can't wait to show you the world of Eternity!
